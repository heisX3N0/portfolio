I'm a Business Analyst and Project Manager in Financial Inclusion at Access Bank in Lagos. I lead requirements end to end, from discovery workshops and stakeholder interviews to signed-off requirements and SIT/UAT, while also running delivery, two roles the Bank normally separates. I'm a Certified Business Analysis Professional (CBAP) and a Professional Scrum Product Owner II (PSPO II), with 4 years across banking, payments and AI products.

Outside the Bank I build AI products through my studio, Version Phi: Aimery, an AI assistant that tailors a resume to a job posting, and Merge X, a mail merge app for Gmail. I'm also Product Lead on GPT Investor, and I'm studying for an MBA in Strategic Management at Babcock University.
